## 3. Physical & Computational Architecture Summary

The finalized DreamBeesAI artifact consists of:
- **Control Plane:** 10-state lifecycle engine, job ledger runner, and autonomous reconciliation loop running in Node.js 22 LTS / TypeScript.
- **Display Gateway:** RFB-over-WebSocket multiplexer bridging browser noVNC canvases directly to container X11VNC servers.
- **Container Runtime:** Hardened Ubuntu 24.04 image with XFCE4, Xvfb, Google Chrome, and official `@anthropic-ai/claude-code` and `@openai/codex` CLIs.
- **Multi-User POSIX Security:** Individual Linux UIDs (`operator` 1000, `collab_1` 1001) with private `chmod 700` home directories and a shared `chmod 2775` setgid workspace directory.
- **Economic Accounting:** Second-by-second tracking of active compute, idle reserve fees ($0.01/hr), and persistent storage ($0.15/GiB-month).
