🔐 Bring Your Own Account (BYOA) Architecture

DreamBees provisions the computer; you bring your own AI agent accounts. Model inference tokens are billed directly by Anthropic or OpenAI under your own quota.

Your API credentials and browser OAuth sessions remain strictly inside your private home directory (/home/<user>, permissions chmod 700).

# Credentials stored securely in your private home folder:
~/.claude.json # Claude Code authentication
~/.codex/auth.json # OpenAI Codex authentication

Other collaborators and tenants cannot read your private home folder or steal your keys.
